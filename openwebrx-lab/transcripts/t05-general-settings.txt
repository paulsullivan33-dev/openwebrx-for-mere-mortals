t05 - general settings, saved via the form
The General settings form must be submitted
whole: a partial POST is rejected and the
form is shown again (HTTP 200, nothing
saved). The lab reposted every field at its
current value, changing four:

    receiver_name     Lab Receiver
    receiver_location Test Bench
    receiver_admin    rowan@example.com
    audio_compression none  (was adpcm)
    fft_compression   none  (was adpcm)

Compression was switched off on purpose:
with raw streams, the test client in t07
can read the waterfall numbers and the
demodulated audio directly instead of
having to decode ADPCM first. Browsers are
equally happy either way.

Evidence it landed:

    settings.json now contains
      "receiver_name": "Lab Receiver"
      "audio_compression": "none"
      "fft_compression": "none"

    GET /status.json ->
      receiver: Lab Receiver | Test Bench
